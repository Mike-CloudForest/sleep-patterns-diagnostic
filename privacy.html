Privacy and your report

Updated November 24, 2026

This page explains how the Sleep Patterns app handles assessment answers, optional observations, newsletter choices and report requests.

While you use the assessment

Your sleep answers, selected tradition profiles, derived chart results and diary entries are kept in this browser tab’s session storage so you can resume after a refresh. Closing the tab normally ends that session, although browser session restoration can bring it back.

Your birth date, local birth time and birthplace are used in the current page to calculate your Chinese birth-year profile, Western Sun and Moon signs, and Human Design chart. The full birth inputs remain in page memory while you work. A refresh clears those inputs unless you later retrieve a profile you explicitly saved with Cloud Forest. The PDF includes derived chart results and calculation notes, without the full birth date, clock time or birthplace.

The birthplace list is a static city-and-time-zone dataset downloaded from this website. City searches run in your browser. The chart also calculates locally; no birth details are automatically sent to an outside astrology service. The optional Jovian Archive link opens a separate service with its own policies.

Choosing Download PDF or Print report creates your copy in the browser. Your browser and device control where a downloaded or printed copy is saved.

When you request an emailed report

The app sends your answers, selected traditions, derived chart results, diary entries, email address, optional name and consent choices to the report service. The service processes them to create and email your PDF.

The app database retains your email address, any name you provide, report-request and delivery records, consent text and version, consent timestamps, and newsletter status. A newsletter opt-in also records the name you typed as your consent signature.

If you leave the separate saved-profile choice unchecked, assessment answers, chart results and diary contents are processed for the email attachment without being written to the app database. Full birth inputs are omitted from that report-only request. A protected request fingerprint is retained to recognize repeat submissions and help prevent duplicate emails.

When you choose to save a profile

The saved-profile choice starts unchecked and is separate from the newsletter. Choosing it allows Cloud Forest to retain your birth details, calculated chart, sleep and everyday-rhythm answers, diary and assessment version, linked to your contact record. The authorized Cloud Forest administrator can review the saved assessment to understand the information you chose to share.

A first saved assessment remains pending until you open the private confirmation link sent with your report. That link verifies access to the email address. Pending links and assessments expire after seven days; expired pending records are removed during later saved-profile activity. Confirmed assessments remain available until you delete the saved profile or arrange deletion with Cloud Forest.

You can return through Saved profiles, request a private sign-in link, review assessment history and save an updated assessment. Recovery links expire after 15 minutes and can be used once. Signed-in sessions last four hours, with their private credentials held in the current tab’s session storage.

Deleting the saved profile removes saved assessments, birth inputs and chart history from the live application database and revokes profile sessions. Contact, consent, newsletter preference and report-delivery records remain. Existing emails, downloads, printed copies and service backups follow their own retention processes. Deleting a saved profile does not change your newsletter preference.

Services that handle the information

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase runs report processing and stores contact, consent and delivery records, plus profiles that visitors explicitly choose to save. Hosting and processing services may keep technical request logs for operation, security and troubleshooting. The app’s own error logging uses general messages and avoids recording birth inputs, answers and diary text.

Resend receives your recipient address, email message and PDF attachment to send the report. It retains sent-email content, attachments and delivery or log data under its service policies. The email service may record delivery and engagement events, such as opens or link clicks, according to its settings.

A report also remains in the recipient’s mailbox according to that email provider’s policies and the recipient’s choices. Provider retention periods can differ from the app’s records.

Newsletter choices

The newsletter choice starts unchecked. Subscribing requires selecting it and typing your full name to sign that consent. You can request an emailed report, download a PDF or use the assessment without subscribing.

The preferences link in your report email opens a confirmation page. Confirming there updates the newsletter preference for this app. The stored unsubscribe record helps Cloud Forest honor that preference.

Access and retained records

Administrator access is limited to the authorized Cloud Forest email account through an expiring sign-in link. The administrator can review contact, consent and report-delivery records, inspect confirmed saved assessments, and export contact lists. The contact CSV does not include birth details or assessment answers. Administrator session credentials are kept in that browser tab’s session storage.

The service uses hashed network and email values for request limits. Contact, consent and delivery records are retained to manage requested reports, newsletter preferences and consent history. You can contact Cloud Forest to ask about, correct or request deletion of your records. We will review the request and any records that need to be retained.

For questions or a request about your information, email sifu@cloudforesttemple.com.
